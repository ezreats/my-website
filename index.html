Our story

It started with one pasta dinner.

My name is Eli Zev, and I'm a sophomore at Staples High School in Westport, Connecticut. After a pasta dinner with my soccer team, we brought the extra food to the Gillespie Center. That night made me realize how much perfectly good food goes unused while people in our own town are facing food insecurity.

Every night, restaurants are left with high-quality, safe food that can't be served the next day. At the same time, many local community members don't have consistent access to fresh, filling meals. EZR Eats exists to responsibly connect those two realities — redistributing unused food directly to local centers the same night it's prepared.

We're a small, student-run club with a faculty advisor and a big goal: make sure good food finds people who need it, safely and every night we can.

How it works

Complete service — we package, pick up, and deliver.

Your restaurant's role is simple: set food aside. We handle everything else, the same night.

1

Set food aside

At closing or throughout the day, set aside any prepared leftover, unserved, or safely stored food. No extra prep, no extra cooking — only food that would otherwise go unused.

2

We pick up & package

Student volunteers arrive at a scheduled, agreed-upon time, package the food into a clearly labeled EZR Eats box, and keep the pickup quick and non-disruptive to your staff.

3

Same-night delivery

Food is transported directly to a local community center or shelter that same night — while it's still fresh and ready to serve.

Safety and responsibility come first. All food is delivered the same night, the program operates with adult oversight and a faculty advisor, and we follow clear handling and labeling guidelines throughout. Participation is completely voluntary and flexible — we work around what's easiest for your restaurant.
